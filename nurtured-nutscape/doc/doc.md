Our creation is a two dimensional platform game that brings awareness to deforestation. 
In our game, the main character, Seeker the Squirrel is trying to provide food for his daughter Seraphina. 
The issue is due to deforestation, Seeker’s task to collect nuts is getting more arduous everyday. 
The player’s goal is to climb high trees to find sevon nuts resting on protruding branches. 
Just when you thought things were at their peak difficulty, you were wrong. 
Sometimes Seeker may not be able to make certain jumps so he will have to be able to push bird nests to give himself a boost.
You have to be careful though, you don’t want the nest to fall off the branch.
All you have to do is bring the nuts back to the squirrel den. 
Besides the obvious reasons, this game is fun and engaging because you have to be careful, navigate your way through the trees, and get the job done.
